Simulated practice

Practise trading with money that isn't real

You get $10,000 of practice cash and a made-up market to try buying and selling, one simulated day at a time.

Practice market A2 fictional companies, 120 days
Practice market B12 fictional companies in 6 sectors, 250 days (Step 4)

How this works

  1. Pick a tickerChoose one of the fictional practice companies.
  2. Preview and confirmSee the estimated cost first. Nothing happens until you confirm.
  3. Advance a dayYour order fills at the next day's opening price. Then see how it went.

Prices are generated by a computer program and are not real. Fees, spreads and slippage are not modelled. Your practice is saved in this browser only; clearing site data removes it.
