Our plain summary

What it does
It starts in cash. It buys when the 20-day average of closing prices crosses above the 50-day average, and sells when it crosses back below.
Why people test it
It is a simple way to follow a trend: stay in while recent prices run above older ones.
Watch out
Averages lag, so it enters and leaves after a move has started. In choppy prices it can buy high and sell low again and again.

How this test works
  • Decisions are made after each day's close, using only prices up to that day. They fill at the next day's opening price, the same rule as your practice orders.
  • It starts with $10,000.00 in cash and buys as many whole shares as the cash allows, then sells all of them on the way out. It never borrows, so cash never goes below zero.
  • Example costs: each fill is 0.10% worse than the opening price. No commission.
  • Days 1–50 are price history used only to build the 50-day average. Nothing is invested and no return is counted for them; the test and buy and hold both start at the Day 50 close.
  • Only days you have reached in Practice Trading are used. Your practice account, orders and cash are not changed.
