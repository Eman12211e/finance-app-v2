Our plain summary

What it shows
Compares the size of recent gains with recent losses on a scale from 0 to 100.
How people read it
Readings above 70 are often called "overbought" and below 30 "oversold": recent moves have been one-sided.
Watch out
Prices can stay above 70 or below 30 for a long time. A high reading is not a forecast that prices will fall.

Education only. An indicator describes past simulated prices; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell.
