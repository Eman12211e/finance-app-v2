SMA 20

Simulated

The close of $199.96 is above its 20-day average of $189.99.

Our plain summary

What it shows
The average closing price of the last 20 simulated days. It smooths out daily ups and downs so the general direction is easier to see.
How people read it
Some people watch whether the price is above or below the average, or whether a short average crosses a longer one.
Watch out
It is built only from past prices, so it always lags. It does not tell you what the next price will be.

EMA 12

Our plain summary

What it shows
An average where recent days count more than older ones, so it reacts sooner when prices change direction.
How people read it
People often compare a fast EMA with a slower average to see whether recent prices are moving away from the longer trend.
Watch out
Reacting sooner also means more false turns when prices bounce around.

Education only. An indicator describes past simulated prices; it is not a recommendation to buy or sell.

Our plain summary

What it shows
The difference between a 12-day and a 26-day EMA (the MACD line), with a 9-day EMA of that difference (the signal line). The bars show the gap between them.
How people read it
Below zero means the shorter average is under the longer one. People watch for the MACD line crossing its signal line.
Watch out
Crossings are frequent and many reverse soon after. Because it is built from averages, it reacts after prices have already moved.
